Bolt & Pip

Short, safe science answers for curious kids.

Bolt is a friendly robot and Pip is his little bird sidekick. Every day they answer one big “why?” question in under a minute: why is the sky blue, why do cats purr, why does ice float, and hundreds more.

Every video is original, animated by hand-built cartoon characters, reviewed by a real person before it is published, and marked “made for kids” on YouTube. No ads inside the videos, no links, no requests for likes or subscriptions, and every experiment says “ask a grown-up” first.
